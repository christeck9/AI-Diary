# Privacy Policy for AI Diary - Private Journal
*Last Updated: 2026-06-01* published at https://rentry.co/y8e2r9xx

This Privacy Policy describes how **Christeck** ("we," "us," or "our") handles user and device data in the **AI Diary - Private Journal** mobile application (the "App") for Android and iOS. 

We take your privacy very seriously. The App is designed with an **offline-first and local-only architecture**, meaning your personal data, journal entries, and recordings never leave your device.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### A. Journal Entries and Text Data
* All notes, journal entries, titles, and thoughts you write in the App are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. 
* We **do not** collect, store, or transmit your journal entries to any external servers.

### B. Voice Recordings and Audio Data
* The App uses your device's microphone to allow voice-to-text journaling.
* All audio transcription (using Whisper) and speech synthesis are executed **100% locally on your device**.
* We **do not** collect, listen to, or upload your audio files, voice recordings, or transcripts to any remote servers.

### C. Local AI Model Processing
* The App features built-in artificial intelligence (Gemma 3 / Gemma 4) to assist you with journaling. 
* All AI processing and text generation are performed **locally on your device’s hardware** (offline inference).
* No conversations, prompts, or AI outputs are sent to us or any third-party AI providers.

---

## 2. Device Permissions

To provide its core features, the App may request access to the following device functions:
* **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** Necessary to record voice notes and translate speech to text.
* **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):** Necessary to keep you notified about the background status of the local AI engine.
* **Storage (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE):** Necessary to allow you to backup or export your journal entries to your device storage.
* **Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE):** Necessary to ensure the local AI engine completes your processing requests without being terminated by the operating system.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services
* **No Data Sharing:** Because the App runs entirely offline, we do not share any personal, behavioral, or device data with third parties.
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** The App does not contain any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or user tracking code.

---

## 4. Data Security
Your data is stored in the private storage directories of the App on your device, managed by the Android and iOS operating systems. We recommend securing your mobile device with a passcode, PIN, or biometric lock (FaceID/Fingerprint) to prevent unauthorized local access to your diary.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since all processing and storage are local to the device, we do not have access to any user data.
